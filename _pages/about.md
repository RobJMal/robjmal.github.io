> *"Ancora Imparo (Still, I am learning)."* — Michelangelo

I am a full-stack **Robotics Engineer** with **3+ years of experience** across industry and research. My expertise lies at the intersection of high-performance mechatronics and intelligent control. 

Currently, I am a Master's student at **ETH Zurich**, wrapping up my thesis at **mimic robotics** and the **Soft Robotics Lab**. My work focuses on benchmarking and designing retargeting algorithms for **humanoid hand control**.

Before ETH, I spent **2 years at UBTECH Robotics** as a Mechatronics Engineer, where I developed hardware for elderly care and rehabilitation robotics. I hold a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from **Harvard University** (with a Minor in Mandarin Chinese). 

### Core Interests
* **Robotic Systems Development:** Designing robust, scalable robotic systems to bridge the gap between initial proof-of-concept and production-ready.
* **Learning-Based Control:** Utilizing reinforcement learning and imitation learning to solve high-dimensional robot control problems. 
* **Mechatronics:** Synthesizing hardware and software, with a focus on wearable and data-collection devices.
